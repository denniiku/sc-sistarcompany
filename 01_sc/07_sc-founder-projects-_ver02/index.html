TRACK I • AI & ENTERPRISE INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS (01–05)

Project 01 | Qubit ERP (intelligence-core / qubit-biz)

1. WHAT IT IS

AI-Native ERP & Shared Logistics

A zero-license-cost Google Workspace AI ERP automating 8 core departments, integrated with CCTV Computer Vision stock counting (Stock Opname) and neighborhood warehouse/delivery sharing (SISTAR SS / DT).

2. WHY WE DO IT

Solving ERP Costs & 3PL Inefficiency

Traditional ERPs (SAP/Oracle) charge excessive per-seat fees while SMEs and veterinary clinics lose margins to manual stock errors, expiring inventory, and fragmented small-parcel shipping costs.

3. HOW IT WORKS

8-Team AI + Vision Opname + SS/DT

Automates Finance (P&L, K-SURE BNPL, KR/ID VAT harvesting), HR (25th profit-sharing payouts), Sales/CRM, and RA. CCTV + Gemini Vision counts warehouse stock in real time, while SS/DT pools idle shop storage and zone deliveries to cut shipping costs by 40%.

4. REVENUE MODEL & EXPECTED ROI

100% Internal Savings + B2B SaaS

Internal ROI: Eliminates 100% of enterprise ERP license and back-office admin payroll across 8 *SC bases.
External Revenue: Monthly B2B SaaS subscriptions (packaged with Zoho Inventory/CRM bridges) for SMEs/clinics + 10–15% shared logistics clearing fees.

Project 02 | Company RAG (intelligence-core)

1. WHAT IT IS

Overnight Data Factory & Dual Vector DB

An unmanned overnight data-harvesting refinery (10 PM–8 AM) paired with a dual-engine vector database (Google NotebookLM + Dify/Supabase pgvector) and standardized Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoints.

2. WHY WE DO IT

Eliminating AI Hallucination in Trade

Generic LLMs fail in real cross-border commerce because they lack live wholesale pricing, Indonesian BPOM/NIE regulations, HS-code tariff updates, and proprietary corporate context.

3. HOW IT WORKS

Nightly Scraping → 8 AM Brief & MCP

Headless bots (Playwright/Apify) scrape 1688, Alibaba, Shopee, Tokopedia, and tariff rules overnight, embedding them alongside internal Google Drive/Keep memos to deliver 8:00 AM executive briefs and ground ask_scarlet.ps1 fact-checking.

4. REVENUE MODEL & EXPECTED ROI

Turnkey RAG Builds + MCP API Fees

Internal ROI: Automated landed-cost, margin, and regulatory verification before any deal execution.
External Revenue: Custom enterprise RAG pipeline setup fees for law/tax/trading firms + automated MCP Server API and x402 micropayment tolls from external AI agents.

TRACK II • HIGH-MARGIN COMMERCIAL & PHYSICAL VERTICALS (06–09)

Project 06 | Smart ODM & Auto-Formulator R1 (biz-hub / biz-galaxy)

1. WHAT IT IS

30L Compounding Robot & Hybrid ODM

Unites our 30-liter autonomous liquid/gel compounding robot (Physio-Formulator R1) and modular robotics (Robot Qubit, Lego Qubit, Aquasis) with a Dual-Stream Korea-Indonesia GMP ODM pipeline.

2. WHY WE DO IT

Breaking MOQ & Tariff Barriers

Traditional ODM factories demand 5,000–10,000+ unit MOQs, while exporting finished liquid bottles to ASEAN incurs heavy import tariffs and freight costs that block mass-market adoption.

3. HOW IT WORKS

Stream A (KR CBU) + Stream B (Rp 1,320)

R1 compounds 30L micro-batches with zero human error. Stream A exports finished premium SKUs from 50-year Korean GMP factories; Stream B exports bulk concentrates to PT. SISTAR Indonesia for local GMP single-dose sachet repacking at Rp 1,320 per sachet.

4. REVENUE MODEL & EXPECTED ROI

50% COGS Cut + Co-Making ODM & HaaS

Internal ROI: Cuts proprietary brand COGS by 50% and eliminates MOQ lockup (>45% gross margin floor).
External Revenue: Co-Making ODM contract manufacturing ("First 3 Shipments 50% Deal") + Hardware-as-a-Service (HaaS) leasing of R1 robots and consumable cartridges to vet hospitals and pharmacies.

4-Model B2G / B2B / B2C Deployment

Maps user inputs to certified scales (S-CTI, PHQ-9, GAD-7) across 4 models: Model A (School Student Dashboards & Parent App), Model B/C (Senior & Clinic Monitoring), and Model D (Corporate HR Employee Assistance Program - EAP).

4. REVENUE MODEL & EXPECTED ROI

Recurring SaaS + Ayurvedic Cross-Sells

Internal ROI: Temperament matching and daily upskilling (+10 curriculum updates/day) for *SC Challengers.
External Revenue: Monthly subscriptions from schools/education boards (B2G), corporate HR (B2B EAP), and parents (B2C) + automated in-app cross-selling of SISTAR AT Ayurvedic herbal adaptogens and NRT kits.
